01Goal

I designed this project for a friend of mine who lives in Canada. Because I couldn't give it to him in person, and shipping costs a lot, I had to solve a number of problems to make it.

02How I designed it

The whole monowire gimmick is that it can retract, similar to a tape measure.

I used the same design approach as the Mantis Blades: I got measurements at different points on my friend's forearm and used them to shape the shell that sits on it. This time I added tolerance, a lesson from the Mantis Blades, where the first version barely fit and kept popping off.

I also made the design print in place. A pin taken out of the brace keeps it locked, which keeps corrections to a minimum while it still works.
