Quadruped Robot

A four-legged robot I'm building one leg at a time. The first test leg has three servos, for roll, hip and knee, and runs the step cycle the robot will use to turn in place.

  • ESP32
  • MG996R servos
  • C++
  • Gait design

01Goal

After the biped, I wanted a robot that stands on its own without active balance, so I can focus on gait. Four legs give it a stable base; three joints per leg let it walk, turn and adjust its height.

02How it works

  • The leg. Three servos: roll swings the leg sideways, the hip lifts it, and the knee bends it.
  • Turning in place. Each leg loops four phases. Lift the foot, swing it toward the turn while it's in the air, plant it, then push it back to center. The push turns the body, because the planted foot can't slide.
  • Power. The servos run from a separate 5 to 6 V supply that shares ground with the ESP32. Each MG996R can pull 1.5 to 2.5 A under load, far more than the board's 5 V pin can give.

03Next

Mount the leg, tune the joint angles to its real range of motion, then build the other three and bring over what I learned balancing the biped.
