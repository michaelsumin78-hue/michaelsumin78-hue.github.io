01Goal

My first Cyberpunk build: wearable arm blades that light up with animated effects.

02How it works

  • Controller. An ESP32 runs FastLED animations on addressable LEDs inside the blades.
  • Power. USB, with a brightness cap in firmware to keep current in range.
  • Trigger. It still runs on a flex sensor, which saves on money.
