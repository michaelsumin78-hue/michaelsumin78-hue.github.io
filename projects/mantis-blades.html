01Goal

My first Cyberpunk build: wearable arm blades that light up with animated effects.

02How it works

  • Controller. An ESP32 runs FastLED animations on addressable LEDs inside the blades.
  • Power. USB, with a brightness cap in firmware to keep current in range.
  • Trigger. I designed an EMG and force-sensor trigger path so a muscle flex could fire the effect.

03Next

Move it to battery power and link it to the Sandevistan spine over ESP-NOW so the two props trigger each other.
